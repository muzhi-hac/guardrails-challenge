## Tariff overview

Last reviewed: 2026-01-01

| Tariff | Price per month | Data allowance |
|---|---|---|
| Tariff S | 19.99 EUR | 5 GB |
| Tariff M | 29.99 EUR | 20 GB |
| Tariff L | 49.99 EUR | 100 GB |

Every tariff includes a flat rate for calls and text messages to all German
networks.

## Changing tariff

Last reviewed: 2026-01-01

You may move to a higher tariff at any time, with effect from the next working
day. A move to a lower tariff takes effect at the end of the billing month, and
is possible at the earliest after 3 months on your current tariff.
