## Home move service

Last reviewed: 2026-01-01

You take your contract with you to your new address. For moving the connection
we charge a one-off fee of 29.90 EUR. Your number, your tariff and your
contract term all stay the same.

Report your move in your account under "Moving home" or by telephone through
customer service. We confirm the order in writing and give you the planned
switch-over date.

## Notice we need

Last reviewed: 2026-01-01

Please tell us about your move at least 6 weeks before the switch-over date you
would like. With shorter notice we will do our best to meet your preferred
date, but we cannot guarantee it.

The gap between disconnection at the old address and activation at the new one
is normally no more than 3 working days.

## Special right of cancellation where service is unavailable

Last reviewed: 2026-01-01

If we cannot provide the service agreed in your contract at your new address,
you have a special right of cancellation. The cancellation takes effect at the
end of the month in which you move, and the minimum term does not stand in its
way. In that case the home move fee is not charged.
