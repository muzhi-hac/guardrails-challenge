## Statutory rules on the notice period

Last reviewed: 2026-01-01

This section states the legal position and applies regardless of our own
contract terms.

Under section 56 of the German Telecommunications Act (TKG), the minimum term
of a consumer contract may not exceed 24 months. Once the minimum term has
expired, the contract may be cancelled at any time, and the notice period may
by law be no longer than 1 month. Automatic renewal for a further fixed term is
not permitted.

## Our contract terms

Last reviewed: 2026-01-01

This section describes our own contract policy.

The minimum term of our mobile contracts is 24 months from the day service is
provided. During the minimum term, a notice period of 1 month before the end of
the term applies. If your cancellation reaches us later than that, the contract
ends 1 month after we receive it.

After the minimum term the contract continues indefinitely and may be cancelled
at any time with 1 month's notice. We confirm every cancellation in writing and
state the end date of the contract.

## How to cancel

Last reviewed: 2026-01-01

You can cancel through the "cancel contracts here" button in your account, by
post, or in writing through the contact form. You will receive our confirmation
within 3 working days.
