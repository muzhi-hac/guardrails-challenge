## Reporting a fault

Last reviewed: 2026-01-01

We accept fault reports around the clock: the fault hotline is available 24/7,
as is the fault form in your account. Please have your customer number to hand
and tell us when the problem started.

We confirm every report with a ticket number and come back to you with a first
assessment within 4 hours at the latest.

## Repair deadline

Last reviewed: 2026-01-01

The repair deadline is 48 hours from the time your fault report reaches us.
Within that deadline we either fix the fault or give you a firm date for the
repair.

If an engineer visit is needed, we offer you at least 2 appointment slots
within the repair deadline.

## Credit for longer outages

Last reviewed: 2026-01-01

If the connection is completely unavailable for more than 72 hours, we credit
the monthly charge for each full day of the outage. The credit is applied
automatically to your next bill; you do not need to claim it.
