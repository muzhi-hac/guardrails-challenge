## Roaming within the EU

Last reviewed: 2026-01-01

EU roaming is included in every tariff at no extra charge. Calls, text messages
and data are billed while you are elsewhere in the EU exactly as they are in
Germany, and your tariff's included allowance continues to apply unchanged.

A fair-use limit of 30 GB per month applies to data used elsewhere in the EU.
Once that limit is reached, we charge the regulated maximum price for each
further megabyte. We send you a text message as soon as 80 % of the fair-use
limit has been used.

## Roaming outside the EU

Last reviewed: 2026-01-01

Outside the EU, data costs 0.49 EUR per MB. Your tariff's included allowance
does not apply there.

To protect you against high bills, a monthly spending limit is preset for data
used outside the EU. Once the limit is reached, we suspend data usage until you
release it again in your account. Calls and text messages are not affected. You
can adjust or remove the limit in your account at any time; the change takes
effect from the following working day.
